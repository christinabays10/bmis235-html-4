Aran Islands

The Aran Islands are three small islands at the mouth of Galway Bay: Inis Mór, Inis Meáin and Inis Oírr.

The biggest highlight is Dún Aonghasa, a stone fort built over 3,000 years ago right on the edge of a cliff.

Irish is still spoken every day on the islands, and the best way to explore is by renting a bike.

Aran Islands are a must see as well as the Cliffs of Moher and Guinness Storehouse.

Ireland is a great Country.
