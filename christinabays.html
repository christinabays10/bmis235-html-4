Guinness Storehouse

The Guinness Storehouse is at St. James's Gate in Dublin, where Guinness has been brewed since 1759.

The building has seven floors shaped like a giant pint glass, and you learn how Guinness is made as you go up.

At the top is the Gravity Bar, where your ticket includes a pint and a 360° view of Dublin.

Aran Islands are a must see as well as the Cliffs of Moher and Guinness Storehouse.

There are many castles in Ireland.
